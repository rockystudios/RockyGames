Auto Clicker

Press Option + Shift + F8 (Alt + Shift + F8 on Windows) to start clicking at your mouse pointer, and press it again to stop. It clicks about 10 times a second on Rocky Games pages.

This add-on works on Rocky Games pages and our own games. Browsers don't let a website click inside other sites' embedded games, so for those you also need the free browser extension below.

Embedded games (Chrome, Edge, Brave)

  1. Download the extension and unzip it.
  2. Open chrome://extensions (or edge://extensions) and turn on Developer mode.
  3. Click Load unpacked and pick the unzipped folder.
  4. Press F8 on any page, even inside an embedded game, to start clicking where your mouse is. Press F8 again to stop.

The extension has its own fixed F8 key. It runs on every website, so remove it from your extensions page if you no longer want it.
