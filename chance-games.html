Rocky Games / Chance arcade

Play by the odds.

Three simple games, with the chances and coin changes shown up front. These use Rocky Games virtual coins only; they have no cash value. Wager any whole amount up to your available balance, and results are generated fairly. No real money, hidden odds, or almost-win effects.

Set a small limit for yourself and take a break whenever you like. Need coins? The daily spin and free built-in games are in the shop.

Call it

Pick heads or tails, then flip a fair virtual coin.

Odds: 1 in 2 to win.
Win: gain your wager.
Lose: lose your wager.

Lucky seven

Roll two six-sided dice. The target is a total of seven.

Odds: 6 of 36 rolls (1 in 6).
Win: gain 5× your wager.
Lose: lose your wager.

Prize wheel

Spin eight equal sections: one prize, seven no-prize sections.

Odds: 1 in 8 to win.
Win: gain 7× your wager.
Lose: lose your wager.
